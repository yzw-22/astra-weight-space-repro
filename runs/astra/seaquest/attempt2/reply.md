I use a center-column hunter: stay near columns 5–6, turn toward targets in the current row, and favor diver collection over enemy pursuit. Vertical movement follows a distance-decaying row potential, with strong penalties for nearby hazards. Low oxygen plus an observable carried diver triggers ascent; otherwise upward movement from row 1 is blocked.

The first layer computes three row-code bits, eight target potentials, eight hazard signals, eight row-gated aiming signals, and oxygen/surface/horizontal-position indicators. The second layer forms action scores, turn requests, and corrections for sticky-action drift. This is an untested, hand-designed heuristic—not a verified optimal policy.
