I use a fixed-column interception strategy: stay at column 5, move vertically toward incoming treasure, and avoid enemies conservatively. This concentrates the network’s capacity on reliable interception rather than general two-dimensional navigation.

The first layer has four units per playable row: player presence, direction-aware treasure value, immediate collision danger, and graded advance warning. Negative trail weights distinguish incoming entities from ones that have already passed. The second layer scores up, down, and waiting using discounted treasure attraction, local danger, sticky-action lookahead, and a slight central-row preference. Eight additional units encourage waiting on a treasure’s row. Horizontal actions and fire are suppressed.
