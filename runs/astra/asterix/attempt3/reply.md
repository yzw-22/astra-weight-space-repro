I use a conservative **center-column interception policy**: keep the player at column 5, move vertically toward incoming treasure, and wait in its row. This exploits the guarantee that each row contains at most one entity.

The first layer computes eight player-row indicators, eight trail-aware collectible-treasure indicators, and two sets of eight danger indicators: threats reaching column 5 within one or three horizontal updates. The second layer gates action utilities by player row, combining discounted treasure attraction, strong collision penalties, and two-row lookahead against sticky movement. Additional units favor waiting in a treasure’s row and remaining still when no collectible treasure is present.
