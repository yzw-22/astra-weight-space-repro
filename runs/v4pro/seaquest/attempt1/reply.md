I designed a hand-built policy network with these ideas:

- H1 units 0–3 and 4–7 encode the submarine front cell’s column and row in binary, using channel 0 minus a small channel 4 contribution to suppress the back cell.
- H1 units 8–16 detect danger in each row; units 17–25 detect reward in each row.
- H1 units 26–31 extract bottom-row gauge features, especially “oxygen low” and “diver present”.
- H2 units 0–8 and 9–17 combine player row with danger/reward rows, giving row-specific “current row danger/reward” detectors.
- H2 units 18–27 are one-hot player column detectors for horizontal scanning.
- H2 units 28–31 detect top row, bottom row, surface need, and the upper-half row band.
- W3 implements a reactive policy: surface when oxygen is low and a diver is available, dodge bullets vertically, fire when an enemy is in the current row, move horizontally to collect divers, and otherwise scan vertically/horizontally.
