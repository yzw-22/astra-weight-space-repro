The policy is a hand-built feature-and-threshold controller:

- **First layer** computes player coordinates, presence of dangers and divers, signed horizontal/vertical comparisons between the player and dangers/divers, and two gauge bits (`oxygen < 60`, `diver bar col 8`).
- **Second layer** combines these into concepts: must surface, at top, diver above/below/same-row/left/right/same-column, enemy same-row/left/right/above/below.
- **Output logits** prioritize surfacing, then chasing divers, then shooting enemies in the same row, with a small default rightward bias underwater.
