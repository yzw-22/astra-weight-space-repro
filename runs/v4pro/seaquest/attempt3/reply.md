The policy is a hand-designed reactive controller encoded in the MLP.

Key ideas:
- First-layer row-coincidence units detect whether the player shares a row with danger (`D_r`) or reward (`R_r`).
- Gauge units detect low oxygen and diver presence from the bottom bar.
- Linear difference units compare player/reward row and column to steer toward rewards.
- Row-group orientation units infer facing from the player’s back cell in the trail channel.
- Second-layer units combine these into actions: surface/avoid up, chase divers left/right, move toward rewards vertically, and fire only when an enemy is in the player’s row and the submarine is facing it.
