The policy simply keeps the paddle directly under the ball: if the ball is to the left/right of the paddle, it moves left/right; if aligned, it chooses no-op.

- h1 unit 0 computes `x_ball - x_paddle`.
- h1 units 1 and 2 are thresholded versions used to detect the equality case.
- h2 unit 0 detects `x_ball == x_paddle`.
- h2 unit 1 encodes whether the ball is to the right of the paddle.
- Outputs: no-op is strong when aligned; left/right are strong according to the rightward sign; useless actions are suppressed.
