The policy is a hand-built heuristic:

- **First hidden layer** builds row-wise detectors:
  - one unit for the player row, one for enemy row, one for treasure row;
  - one signed unit per row that reports whether treasure in that row is left/right of the player.
- **Second hidden layer** combines these into:
  - row-specific “enemy in the player’s row” detectors;
  - row-specific “treasure left/right in the player’s row” detectors;
  - global “enemy above/below player” and “treasure above/below player” relations.
- **Output layer**:
  - moves horizontally toward treasure in the current row when safe;
  - escapes vertically when an enemy is in the current row;
  - otherwise moves vertically toward treasure while avoiding enemies in that direction;
  - strongly suppresses no-op and fire.
