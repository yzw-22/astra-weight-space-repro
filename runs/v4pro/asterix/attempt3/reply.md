The strategy is a hand-built heuristic:

- First-layer units detect, for each of the 8 playable rows, the player row, danger presence/side, and gold presence/side.
- Second-layer units combine these into row-specific “danger on my row”, “gold on my row”, “gold to my right”, and coarse vertical attraction/repulsion signals.
- The output layer turns these into action logits: vertical movement is strongly preferred when danger is on the player’s row, otherwise the player moves horizontally toward same-row gold or vertically toward gold/danger half-rows.
