Design: the network tracks the ball, its direction from the trail, and the paddle position through threshold units. When the ball is moving down in the brick-free lower region, it computes the wall-reflected landing column at row 8 (including the one-step “stick” when the ball hits a side wall); otherwise it simply tracks the current ball column. Hidden layer 2 implements the resulting comparisons (landing column greater/less than paddle) and fallback rules; the output layer turns these into near-deterministic left/right/no-op decisions.
