Design summary: the network implements a hand-coded reactive controller. The first hidden layer computes: player row/edge status, oxygen and diver-bar bits, no-bullet availability, net-diver position/direction (using channel2−channel1 so fish/subs cancel), all-reward directions for enemy/diver fallback, danger-row presence, and robust “danger in my current row” detectors for rows 1–8. The second hidden layer forms AND/OR gates for: surface when oxygen is low and a diver is aboard; move toward divers with priority over ordinary rewards; chase ordinary rewards only when no net diver is present; safe upward-only movement that cannot accidentally surface without a diver; current-row danger avoidance; aligned-pickup no-op; and firing only when an enemy is in the current row and no friendly bullet is already present. Output weights are offset by baselines so inactive gates contribute zero; a small default “down” bias keeps the submarine from random-walking into the surface.
