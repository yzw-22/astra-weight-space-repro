Design brief: the network is a hand-built reactive controller. First-layer units compute signed relative coordinates of divers (using the reward minus danger channels), relative danger row/column, danger-count, oxygen gauges, carried-diver/lower-gauge bits, and simple bullet-presence gates. Hidden-layer units form threshold/AND rules for: descend toward visible divers (wait at the safe top row until at least three are visible), move toward the average visible diver, move up to bank six carried divers when oxygen is low, emergency surfacing, close-range firing when an enemy is near on the same row, turning/shooting toward same-row danger, and vertical bullet dodging. Output biases make no-op the safe default and give priority to emergency surfacing, close firing, target movement, then waiting.
