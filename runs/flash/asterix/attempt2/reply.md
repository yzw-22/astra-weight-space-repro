Design: the network is hand-wired as a state machine.  First-layer units detect, for each of the eight playable rows, the player, gold, enemies, and the signed gold column relative to the player.  Hidden-layer units turn these into: (i) same-row gold to the left/right, (ii) same-row enemy danger, and (iii) a vertical gold-seeking score for the player’s current row.  The output layer strongly prefers collecting same-row gold, escapes a row containing an enemy by moving upward (except at the top edge, where it moves down), otherwise moves toward the majority of gold above/below, and defaults to no-op when there is no useful target.  Fire is suppressed.
