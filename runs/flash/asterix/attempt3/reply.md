The policy uses the two hidden layers as a hand-built rule system. Layer 1 encodes, for each row, whether the player is there, whether an entity is present, whether it is enemy/gold, and its horizontal relation to the player. Layer 2 turns these into one advice detector per row for each action: move up/down to avoid an enemy or approach gold above/below; move left/right to collect gold in the current row. The final layer sums the current-row detector into action logits, with no-op strongly favored when no advice fires.
